FIELD GUIDE / HOW TO PLAY

Travel through deep time.

01

Place

Drag the grip on an event card into the timeline, or click a card and then click the canvas. Singular events become pins; ongoing events become editable bars.

02

Navigate

Drag empty canvas to pan. Scroll or pinch to zoom around your pointer, use the + / − buttons, or jump between eras with the presets.

03

Refine together

Drag pins or bars to reposition. Drag either end of a bar to adjust its duration. Shift-click several events, then drag one to move the whole selection.

04

Connect & score

Choose “Link events” and click two placed milestones. Press “Check accuracy” for date, interval, and sequence scoring. You can keep correcting guesses.
